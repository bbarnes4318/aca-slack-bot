# Critical Illness Insurance and Closing the Sale

## Critical Illness Insurance (Ancillary Product)
### What It Covers
- Cancer diagnosis
- Heart attack
- Stroke
- Life insurance component (death benefit to beneficiary)
- **Benefit:** Lump sum payment upon qualifying event

### When to Offer Critical Illness
**Perfect for:**
- High deductible plans
- Bronze plans with $0 premium
- Customers concerned about catastrophic medical bills

### Sales Script Example
"I know this plan has a high deductible, but I have something that pairs perfectly with it - critical illness coverage. It's only $40-50 per month. God forbid you had cancer, heart attack, or stroke, you'd receive a lump sum payment of $[AMOUNT]. You can use that to pay your entire deductible and have money left over for bills if you can't work."

## Closing the Sale
### Initial Close Questions
"Where would you like to have your insurance card mailed to?"

**If premium is not $0:**
"What method of payment would you like to use? Visa or Mastercard?"

### Information Confirmation
"Let me confirm the spelling of your first and last name as it appears on your tax returns..."
"I have your date of birth as [DATE], is that correct?"
"What's your email address?"
"And you are a U.S. citizen, correct?"

## Payment Processing
### For Policies with Premium
"What method of payment would you like to use? Visa or Mastercard?"

### Payment Collection Process
- If premium is due, collect during enrollment
- Use healthcare.gov payment portal
- Click "Set up payment" button
- Process payment immediately for activation

### Zero Premium Policies
- Skip payment method question
- Proceed directly to verification
- Social Security Number becomes even more critical

## Electronic Signature & Agreement
### Statement After Verification
"Thank you. This constitutes your electronic agreement and signature on your application."
