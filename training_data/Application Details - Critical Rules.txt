Training File 16: Carrier Switching Restrictions
# Health Insurance Marketplace Enrollment Training
## Module 6: Application Details - Critical Rules

### Tax Filing Requirements

#### MUST File Jointly if Married:
- **Question:** "Do you plan to file taxes for 2025?"
- **Answer MUST be:** YES
- **If married, MUST select:** Filing jointly
- **If answer NO to either = DISQUALIFIED**

#### Exception Explanation to Customer:
"On healthcare.gov, you must file jointly with your spouse. However, at year-end, we're not accountants. Whatever your accountant decides is best, that's what you do. There's no penalty for not filing jointly, but you're expected to file jointly."

### Household Members
**Customer:** "I just need coverage for myself"
**Agent:** "Are you married? Do you claim dependents?"
**If YES:** "We need to put EVERYBODY on the application"

**Rule:** Everyone on taxes = Everyone in household (Even if not all getting coverage)

### Tobacco Use
**ALWAYS SELECT: NO**

**Reasons:**
- Healthcare.gov has no restrictions
- Can enroll with ANY condition (HIV, cancer, etc.)
- Selecting YES increases premium
- Could eliminate subsidy qualification
- Creates payment collection requirement
