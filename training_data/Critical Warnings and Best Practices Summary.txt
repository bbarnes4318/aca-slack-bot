# Critical Warnings and Best Practices Summary

## Critical Don'ts
❌ **NEVER:**
- Tell customers final price before completing application
- Say YES to tobacco use
- Allow married filing separately
- Enter itemized deductions
- Let customers call back with documents
- Skip household members on taxes
- Submit incomplete applications
- Telling customers they don't qualify without probing
- Skipping the value establishment questions
- Not mentioning deductible/coinsurance/max out-of-pocket
- Using outdated FPL numbers
- Force-feeding plans without understanding needs
- Not educating about future income vs. past income
- Sounding nervous or uncertain when asking for SSN
- Making SSN request sound like a big deal
- Skipping verification process
- Not educating about ancillary products for high-deductible plans
- Cutting corners to save time
- Say "call me back with documents"
- Wait until next day to request
- Skip verification process
- Assume documents will be sent later
- Write Golden Rule policies
- Mislead or steer for financial benefit
- Rush through verification

## Critical Do's
✅ **ALWAYS:**
- Get documents same call
- Select "filing jointly" if married
- Say NO to tobacco
- Use yearly income
- Complete verification on every call
- State "recorded line" at beginning
- Get clear "YES" for consent
- Mention coinsurance, deductible, max out-of-pocket
- Complete verification on EVERY call
- Collect documents same day
- Build confidence before asking for SSN
- Make SSN request conversational and expected
- Use ancillary products to overcome deductible objections
- Complete full verification every time
- Maintain confidence throughout close
- Think of yourself as order-taker, not salesperson
- Be confident when requesting documents
- Save your contact in customer's phone
- Use fear of cancellation to motivate action
- Process payments immediately
- Use current tools - Don't rely on outdated charts
- Focus on 2025 expected income, not past earnings
- Always probe and educate low-income customers
- Ask what they need from their insurance
- State all key financial components of plan
- Send text screenshots while explaining verbally
- Document everything - This protects your license
- Follow the script
- Build rapport
- Be confident
- Educate, don't sell
- Get documents immediately
- Ask for referrals
- Protect your license

## Common Issues & Solutions
### Government Phone Customers:
- 90-day service periods
- Using family/friend's phone
- **Solution:** Get everything NOW

### Price Changes:
"Never give price at beginning - subsidy can change after full application. You can always go down, but can't go up."

### Document Triggers:
- Previous Medicaid application
- Similar name in system
- Income verification needed
- Immigration status

## Success Formula:
1. Follow the script
2. Build rapport
3. Be confident
4. Educate, don't sell
5. Get documents immediately
6. Ask for referrals
7. Protect your license

## Remember:
"This is a traditional sale. If you come from Medicare, remove all fear. 
This is not that type of industry. It's much more relaxed, much less compliance-heavy. 
Everything else is free game."

"These calls are coming in because they're looking for health insurance.
Keep it that way. Make it like you're order taking, customer service.
You're HELPING them, not selling them."
