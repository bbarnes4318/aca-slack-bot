# Complete Verification Process Script

## Purpose
**This protects:**
- Your license
- The agency
- Creates legally binding verbal signature
- Prevents false complaints
- Required on EVERY call

## Complete Verification Script

"Now, before we continue, as I stated in the beginning, this call is being recorded. 
My name is [YOUR NAME] with [AGENCY]. 
Today's date is [DATE]. 

Please state your full name and date of birth for consent to enroll in the marketplace.
[Wait for response]

I'm going to read some disclosures. Please wait to respond until I ask for each of the terms:

You have selected the [CARRIER NAME] [PLAN TYPE] Plan. 
Your monthly premium after the subsidy is $[AMOUNT]. 
Your estimated income is $[AMOUNT], 
and the effective date for this plan is January 1st, 2026.

Do you hereby provide your verbal consent and authorize [AGENCY] to enroll you in the [CARRIER] plan provided through the marketplace? 
Do you agree?
[Wait for YES]

You authorize [AGENCY] to update your health plan and make necessary changes for one year to keep the policy active, request required documentation/extensions, and notify you via call, text or email. 
Do you agree?
[Wait for YES]

You authorize us to auto-renew your policy for plan year 2027 to ensure no lapse in coverage. 
Do you agree?
[Wait for YES]

Now, do you understand that your [CARRIER] policy is separate from any other products you may have discussed or may be purchasing today? 
If you understand and wish to proceed, please confirm by saying yes.
[Wait for YES]

This constitutes your electronic agreement and signature on your application."

## Important Updates
⚠️ **Year Corrections:** Update all references to correct tax years
- 2026 for current enrollment year
- 2027 for auto-renewal year
- **Agency Name:** Update from "InsureACA" to current agency name as needed

## Key Points
- **Confidence is everything**
- Read verification, get the yes, keep it pushing
- This is legally binding
- No shortcuts allowed
