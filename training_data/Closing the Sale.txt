# Health Insurance Marketplace Enrollment Training
## Module 3: Closing the Sale

### Initial Close Questions
"Where would you like to have your insurance card mailed to?"

**If premium is not $0:**
"What method of payment would you like to use? Visa or Mastercard?"

### Information Confirmation
"Let me confirm the spelling of your first and last name as it appears on your tax returns..." "I have your date of birth as [DATE], is that correct?" "What's your email address?" "And you are a U.S. citizen, correct?"

### Obtaining Social Security Numbers

#### Critical Understanding
- Cannot enroll without SSN - It's as critical as getting credit card information
- SSN is how government identifies eligibility for subsidies
- Required for subsidy qualification and payment

#### Key Success Factors for Getting SSN

**1. Confidence is Everything**
- Sound confident and expectant
- Treat it as order-taking, not requesting
- Make it part of natural flow

**2. Effective Approaches**

**Option A - Natural Flow:**
"Are you a U.S. citizen? Great. And your social, please?"

**Option B - Verification Approach:**
"Just to verify your social, please..."

(Implies you already have it, just confirming)

**Option C - Statement Not Question:**
"And your social..."

(Not "What is your social?" but stating expectation)

**3. Building Foundation**
- Good rapport earlier = easier SSN collection
- If you've done your job building trust, this flows naturally

#### Handling SSN Objections
If customer objects:
- Refer back to citizenship verification
- Explain it's how we verify identity

**Compare to other services:**
"This is health insurance. Just like when you get auto insurance from Progressive or State Farm, or even open a Verizon account - the social is required for verification and subsidy approval."

**Key Mindset:**
- In the US, SSN is required for any legitimate business transaction
- This is standard procedure, not special request
- We're providing solutions, this is part of the process
