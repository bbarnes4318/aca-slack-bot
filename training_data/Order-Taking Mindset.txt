# Health Insurance Marketplace Enrollment Training
## Module 6: Order-Taking Mindset

### Key Philosophy
"This is ORDER TAKING, not selling"

#### Remember:
- Calls are coming because they WANT health insurance
- They WANT to sign up for healthcare.gov
- You're customer service, helping them
- Don't make them feel like you're selling

#### Approach:
"Do you have doctors you already see? Let me check that for you. Certain medications? Let me verify coverage. Let me find the RIGHT plan for you."
