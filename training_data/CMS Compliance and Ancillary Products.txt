# Health Insurance Marketplace Enrollment Training

## Module 3: CMS Compliance and Ancillary Products

### Understanding CMS Compliance

#### What is CMS?
- **CMS = Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services**
- They are the "police" of ACA and Medicare
- Function as regulatory enforcement for healthcare enrollment

#### CMS Monitoring Activities
- Secret shoppers ("trappers") make undercover calls
- Test agents on script compliance and proper procedures
- May complete entire enrollment to test process
- **This year expectation:** Increased monitoring and undercover calls

#### Compliance Requirements
**Key Principles:**
- Don't cut corners - more shortcuts = more liability
- Don't mislead customers
- Don't steer customers to certain plans for financial benefit
- Always do what's best for the customer
- You can make money doing it the right way

### Critical Illness Insurance (Ancillary Product)

#### What It Covers
- Cancer diagnosis
- Heart attack
- Stroke
- Life insurance component (death benefit to beneficiary)
- **Benefit:** Lump sum payment upon qualifying event

#### When to Offer Critical Illness
**Perfect for:**
- High deductible plans
- Bronze plans with $0 premium
- Customers concerned about catastrophic medical bills

#### Sales Script Example
"I know this plan has a high deductible, but I have something that pairs perfectly with it - critical illness coverage. It's only $40-50 per month. God forbid you had cancer, heart attack, or stroke, you'd receive a lump sum payment of $[AMOUNT]. You can use that to pay your entire deductible and have money left over for bills if you can't work."
