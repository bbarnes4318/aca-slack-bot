# Initial Qualification and Eligibility Screening

## Required Information Gathering
1. "Are you looking for individual or family plans today?"
2. "In case we get disconnected, what is the best number to reach you?"
3. "Do you have Medicaid, Medicare, VA, Tri-Care, or any other health coverage outside of the marketplace?"

## Disqualification Criteria
### Cannot enroll if customer has:
- Active Medicaid
- Active Medicare
- VA benefits
- Tri-Care

### Exception
If customer received letter stating Medicaid is about to expire, enrollment is permitted

### Can enroll if customer has:
- Employer group coverage (during open enrollment period)
- Must verify they are in their employer's open enrollment period

## Consent Requirements
### MUST obtain clear "YES" for consent
- If customer says "yeah," "sure," or "okay" - politely ask: "Is that a yes?"
- This is critical for compliance

## Verification Script
"Am I speaking with [CUSTOMER NAME]? You want to confirm that's [SPELL NAME]? What is your zip code? Do I have your consent to check the marketplace to verify your information and options? [WAIT FOR YES] Your health information is only for determining your enrollment eligibility and will not be shared with third parties."

## Green Card & Immigration Status
### Eligibility Requirements
✅ **Can enroll if they have:**
- Social Security Number
- Green card
- Valid immigration status

### Documentation Process
If eligibility results show documentation needed:
1. Request immediately on the call
2. "I need you to take a photo of your green card and send it to me right now"
3. Get the appropriate number (A-number, I-94, etc.)
4. Take screenshot/photo while on call
