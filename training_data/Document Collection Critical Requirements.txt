# Health Insurance Marketplace Enrollment Training
## Module 4: Document Collection Critical Requirements

### Timeline Changes
- **OLD:** 90 days to collect documents
- **NEW:** Only 30 days to collect documents
- Policies go into effect January 1st
- **No documents = No coverage = No commission**

### Green Card & Immigration Status

#### Eligibility Requirements
✅ **Can enroll if they have:**
- Social Security Number
- Green card
- Valid immigration status

#### Documentation Process
If eligibility results show documentation needed:
1. Request immediately on the call
2. "I need you to take a photo of your green card and send it to me right now"
3. Get the appropriate number (A-number, I-94, etc.)
4. Take screenshot/photo while on call

### Best Practices for Document Collection

#### During the Call:
"Your application is missing some requested documents from the government. Without this, your policy is at risk of losing your subsidy. I need you to send this to me right now while we're on the phone."

#### Effective Techniques:

**For Written Statements:**
- "Can you grab a piece of paper and pen right now?"
- "Write this statement: [dictate statement]"
- "Take a photo and text/email it to me now"

**For Photo IDs/Documents:**
- "Take a photo with your phone"
- "Text it to me at [number]"
- "I just texted you - save my contact as [Name], Health Insurance Agent"

**Success Rate:** 80% compliance when requested during call

### Follow-up Strategy (If Documents Not Obtained Initially)

#### Next Day Text Message:
"This is [Agent Name], your health insurance agent. Your coverage for you and your family will be CANCELED if you don't send the required documents. You could be charged in full for any medical services. Please send [specific documents] immediately to avoid cancellation."

### Eligibility Letter Review

#### Red Flags to Watch For
If eligibility letter shows:
- Medicaid requested docs
- Medicare requested docs
- VA requested docs

**This typically means:**
- Income is below threshold
- They already have government coverage
- Their SSN is linked to previous application

**What to Do:**
"You need to provide me with a letter showing you are losing your [Medicaid/Medicare]. Without this termination letter, we cannot proceed with enrollment."

### Working with Government Phone Customers

#### Common Issues
- Government-assisted cell phones (90-day service)
- Using family/friend's phone for application
- Difficult to reach after call ends

#### Solutions
- Get all documents during initial call
- Save your number in their contacts immediately
- Get alternative contact numbers
- Send reminder texts same day

### Document Frequency
- Approximately 1-2 out of 10 clients require documents
- Usually triggered by:
  - Previous Medicaid application
  - Similar name to existing application
  - Income verification needs
  - Non-citizen status

### Remember
"Confidence is everything. Read verification, get the yes, keep it pushing." "The best time to get a document from a customer is while you have them on the phone"
