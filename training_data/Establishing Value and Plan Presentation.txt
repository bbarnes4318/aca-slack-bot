# Health Insurance Marketplace Enrollment Training
## Module 2: Establishing Value and Plan Presentation

### Establishing Value

#### Purpose
- Shows you care about customer needs
- Increases likelihood of enrollment completion and retention

#### Value Discovery Script
"What are you looking for in a health insurance plan?"

**Follow-up probing questions:**
- "Are there any medications that you must have covered?"
- "Do you feel pain anywhere?"
- "Is there anything specific you need your insurance for?"
- "Are you just looking for regular doctor visits?"

**Don't accept:** "Nothing really, I just want coverage" - PROBE DEEPER

#### Transition to Plan Search
"Thank you for the information. One moment while I search the marketplace for you."

### Notification of Subsidy

#### Subsidy Approval Script
"Great news! You've been approved for a federal subsidy of $[AMOUNT], which will be applied directly to your policy premium today."

#### Best Practice
- Take screenshot of plan
- Send as text message to customer
- Say: "Have you received the text of the policy on your phone? Let's review it together."

### Outline of Benefits Review

#### Required Components (CMS Mandate)
You MUST mention ALL of the following:
- Coinsurance percentage
- Deductible amount
- Maximum out-of-pocket amount

#### Benefits Review Script
"I found a great plan with [CARRIER NAME]. This plan includes all of your preventive care such as:
Your annual checkup
Colonoscopy
Your blood work and lab work
All your regular checkup visits ...which are fully covered at 100%.
Now, if you need to see the doctor during the year for anything else:
Your primary doctor copay is just $[AMOUNT]
Your specialist copay is only $[AMOUNT]
Your deductible is $[AMOUNT]
Your coinsurance is [PERCENTAGE]%
Your maximum out-of-pocket is $[AMOUNT]"

### Compliance Alert
⚠️ **CMS VIOLATION WARNING:** Failure to mention coinsurance, deductible, and maximum out-of-pocket can result in:
- Agent violations
- License actions
- Compliance infractions

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
❌ Telling customers they don't qualify without probing
❌ Skipping the value establishment questions
❌ Not mentioning deductible/coinsurance/max out-of-pocket
❌ Using outdated FPL numbers
❌ Force-feeding plans without understanding needs
❌ Not educating about future income vs. past income
