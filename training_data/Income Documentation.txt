Training File 17: Income Documentation Best Practices

# Health Insurance Marketplace Enrollment Training
## Module 6: Income Documentation

### Income Entry Best Practices

#### Preferred Method:
- Use YEARLY income (not hourly/monthly)
- Simplest and quickest approach
- Reduces calculation errors

#### Critical Distinction:
"You could be self-employed making $300,000/year. All that matters is your NET income. Remember your adjusted gross income on your tax return? That's all they care about."

### Deductions Handling

**When asked about deductions:**
**ALWAYS SELECT: NO**

**Reasoning:**
- You're not an accountant
- Too complicated to itemize
- Risk of errors
- Use their adjusted gross income ÷ 12 months
- They're giving you their average

### Special Populations

#### High-Income Scenarios
**If income > $100,000 and kids on Medicaid/CHIP:**
"When we enter $100,000/year, your kids will no longer qualify for Kid Care. We need to find a plan for everyone.

**Opportunity:** More enrollments, better coverage
"Kids on Medicaid are limited to certain doctors. Blue Cross Blue Shield network offers more options."

#### Documentation Requirements by Population
- **American Indian/Alaska Native:** Select NO (don't qualify for healthcare.gov)
- **Incarcerated individuals:** Don't qualify for healthcare.gov
- **Hispanic/Latino question:** Optional - can skip (select decline)

### Application Settings

#### Communication Preferences
**Paper notices: ALWAYS select "Send by mail"**

**Reason:**
"Many people don't check email/texts, think it's spam, might miss critical coverage letters"

Hard copy = "They can't say 'I never got it'"
