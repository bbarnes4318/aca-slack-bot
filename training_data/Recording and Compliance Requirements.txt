# Health Insurance Marketplace Enrollment Training
## Module 1: Recording and Compliance Requirements

### Mandatory Recording Disclosure
- **ALWAYS** state at the beginning of every call: "We are on a recorded line"
- This is a legal requirement for telemarketing
- Never skip this disclosure

### Standard Opening Script
"Hello, this is [AGENT NAME] with InsureACA. I'm a registered agent with the marketplace. We are on a recorded line. My job is to help you understand your health options and see if you qualify for government subsidies which help save on your health insurance."

### Initial Qualification Questions
**Required Information Gathering:**
- "Are you looking for individual or family plans today?"
- "In case we get disconnected, what is the best number to reach you?"
- "Do you have Medicaid, Medicare, VA, Tri-Care, or any other health coverage outside of the marketplace?"

### Disqualification Criteria
**Cannot enroll if customer has:**
- Active Medicaid
- Active Medicare
- VA benefits
- Tri-Care

**Exception:** If customer received letter stating Medicaid is about to expire, enrollment is permitted

**Can enroll if customer has:**
- Employer group coverage (during open enrollment period)
- Must verify they are in their employer's open enrollment period

### Consent Requirements
- MUST obtain clear "YES" for consent
- If customer says "yeah," "sure," or "okay" - politely ask: "Is that a yes?"
- This is critical for compliance

### Verification Script
"Am I speaking with [CUSTOMER NAME]? You want to confirm that's [SPELL NAME]? What is your zip code? Do I have your consent to check the marketplace to verify your information and options? [WAIT FOR YES] Your health information is only for determining your enrollment eligibility and will not be shared with third parties."

## CMS Compliance Overview
### What is CMS?
- **CMS = Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services**
- They are the "police" of ACA and Medicare
- Function as regulatory enforcement for healthcare enrollment

### CMS Monitoring Activities
- Secret shoppers ("trappers") make undercover calls
- Test agents on script compliance and proper procedures
- May complete entire enrollment to test process
- **This year expectation:** Increased monitoring and undercover calls

### Compliance Requirements
**Key Principles:**
1. Don't cut corners - more shortcuts = more liability
2. Don't mislead customers
3. Don't steer customers to certain plans for financial benefit
4. Always do what's best for the customer
5. You can make money doing it the right way

## Critical Compliance Points
- Document everything on recorded line for license protection
- Get clear verbal consent before proceeding
- Complete verification on EVERY call
- No exceptions to compliance requirements
