# Outline of Benefits Review Requirements

## Required Components (CMS Mandate)
**You MUST mention ALL of the following:**
1. Coinsurance percentage
2. Deductible amount
3. Maximum out-of-pocket amount

## Benefits Review Script
"I found a great plan with [CARRIER NAME]. This plan includes all of your preventive care such as:
- Your annual checkup
- Colonoscopy
- Your blood work and lab work
- All your regular checkup visits
...which are fully covered at 100%.

Now, if you need to see the doctor during the year for anything else:
- Your primary doctor copay is just $[AMOUNT]
- Your specialist copay is only $[AMOUNT]
- Your deductible is $[AMOUNT]
- Your coinsurance is [PERCENTAGE]%
- Your maximum out-of-pocket is $[AMOUNT]"

## Compliance Alert
⚠️ **CMS VIOLATION WARNING:** Failure to mention coinsurance, deductible, and maximum out-of-pocket can result in:
- Agent violations
- License actions
- Compliance infractions

## Plan Selection Best Practices
### Always check:
1. Primary care copay
2. Specialist copay
3. Drug coverage (90% of drugs are generic = $3 copay)
4. Blood work costs (many people get twice yearly)
5. Urgent care copay
6. Emergency room costs

## Understanding "Full Price"
"Full price" = Customer pays entire amount until deductible is met
**Example:** $3,500 deductible = First $3,500 of ER bill is customer's responsibility
