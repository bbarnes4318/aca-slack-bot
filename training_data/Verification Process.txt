# Health Insurance Marketplace Enrollment Training
## Module 3: Verification Process

### Purpose
This protects:
- Your license
- The agency
- Creates legally binding verbal signature
- Prevents false complaints
- **Required on EVERY call**

### Best Practices
✅ Make SSN request conversational and expected
✅ Use ancillary products to overcome deductible objections
✅ Complete full verification every time
✅ Maintain confidence throughout close
✅ Think of yourself as order-taker, not salesperson

### Remember
"The agents who get the most sales are the ones who sound the most confident when asking for the social. It's not a request - it's part of the process."
