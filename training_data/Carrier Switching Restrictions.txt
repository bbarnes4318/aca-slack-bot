# Health Insurance Marketplace Enrollment Training
## Module 6: Carrier Switching Restrictions

### Non-Payment Situations
⚠️ **CRITICAL:** Some carriers don't pay for switches

#### Florida Blue Specific Rule:
- If customer has Florida Blue in January
- You enroll them in another plan with Florida Blue
- **NO COMMISSION PAID**

#### Carriers that Restricted Switches (2024):
- Ambetter
- United Healthcare
- **(Note: Restrictions may be lifted for 2025 due to new fraud protections)**

### Application Process in Serpa

#### Starting Application
1. Quote customer → Hit "Start Application"
2. System automatically searches marketplace for existing applications
3. **If found:** Use existing (pre-filled information)
4. **If not found:** Start new application

#### Using Existing Applications

**Benefits of using existing app:**
- All information carries over
- Eliminates duplicate work
- Faster enrollment process
- Less chance for errors
- Always check for prior year applications first

# Health Insurance Marketplace Enrollment Training
## Module 6: Social Security Number Collection

### Professional Approach Script
"What we're about to do is a government application with healthcare.gov. I'm fully certified with the government - you can look me up. My licenses are available on public information. This will require your full social to link your information with the IRS for your application."

### Building Comfort Before SSN Request

**Ask these questions FIRST:**
1. What state are you in?
2. Are you married?
3. Are you working?
4. How much are you making?
5. Any dependents?

**THEN ask for social** - feels more natural in flow

### Security Assurance
"Once I enter your social, it's encrypted. I won't be able to view it - even as your agent. The system only shows the last four digits. This is a government website with full protection under the Affordable Care Act."
