# Health Insurance Marketplace Enrollment Training
## Module 5: Plan Presentation in Sherpa

### Main Dashboard Navigation
1. Click "Quote on Exchange"
2. Enter customer information:
   - Zip code
   - Household size
   - Income
   - Age

### Building Quote

**Example:**
- Single household
- Income: $20,000
- Age: 47

### Important Household Rules
⚠️ **Critical:** Include ALL household members on taxes, even if not getting coverage

**Customer:** "I just need coverage for myself"
**Agent:** "Are you married? Do you claim any dependents?"
**Customer:** "Yes"
**Agent:** "We need to put everybody on the application"

### Doctor & Prescription Search

#### Left Sidebar Features:

**Doctor search:**
- Type doctor's name
- Check box to filter plans
- Shows only in-network plans

**Prescription search:**
- Enter medications
- Filter by coverage

### Plan Selection Best Practices

**Always check:**
- Primary care copay
- Specialist copay
- Drug coverage (90% of drugs are generic = $3 copay)
- Blood work costs (many people get twice yearly)
- Urgent care copay
- Emergency room costs

### Understanding "Full Price"
"Full price" = Customer pays entire amount until deductible is met Example: $3,500 deductible = First $3,500 of ER bill is customer's responsibility

### Plan Comparison Example

**Plan A: United Zero Premium**
- $0 premium
- $0 deductible
- But: $50 primary care, $120 specialist

**Plan B: Alternative**
- Small premium
- Low deductible
- $10 primary care, $30 specialist

**Customer conversation:**
"I don't really go to the hospital often, I just want low copays when I see the doctor" → Choose Plan B despite higher premium
