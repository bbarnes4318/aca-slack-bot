# Document Collection Critical Requirements

## Timeline Changes
- **OLD:** 90 days to collect documents
- **NEW:** Only 30 days to collect documents
- Policies go into effect January 1st
- **No documents = No coverage = No commission**

## Best Practices for Document Collection

### During the Call:
"Your application is missing some requested documents from the government. 
Without this, your policy is at risk of losing your subsidy. 
I need you to send this to me right now while we're on the phone."

### Effective Techniques:
1. **For Written Statements:**
   - "Can you grab a piece of paper and pen right now?"
   - "Write this statement: [dictate statement]"
   - "Take a photo and text/email it to me now"

2. **For Photo IDs/Documents:**
   - "Take a photo with your phone"
   - "Text it to me at [number]"
   - "I just texted you - save my contact as [Name], Health Insurance Agent"

3. **Success Rate:** 80% compliance when requested during call

## Follow-up Strategy (If Documents Not Obtained Initially)
### Next Day Text Message:
"This is [Agent Name], your health insurance agent. 
Your coverage for you and your family will be CANCELED if you don't send the required documents. 
You could be charged in full for any medical services. 
Please send [specific documents] immediately to avoid cancellation."

## Eligibility Letter Review
### Red Flags to Watch For
If eligibility letter shows:
- Medicaid requested docs
- Medicare requested docs
- VA requested docs

### This typically means:
1. Income is below threshold
2. They already have government coverage
3. Their SSN is linked to previous application

### What to Do:
"You need to provide me with a letter showing you are losing your [Medicaid/Medicare].
Without this termination letter, we cannot proceed with enrollment."

## Document Requirements
⚠️ **No Commission Without Documents**
- Documents must be obtained for coverage to begin
- No documents = No coverage = No pay
- Could result in chargeback if advanced

## Why Speed Matters
1. 30-day window only (down from 90 days)
2. January 1st effective date approaching
3. Customer phone numbers may change (government phones)
4. Callback success rate: "Less than zero"

## Best Time to Get Documents
**"The best time to get a document from a customer is while you have them on the phone"**

## Document Frequency
- Approximately **1-2 out of 10** clients require documents
- Usually triggered by:
  - Previous Medicaid application
  - Similar name to existing application
  - Income verification needs
  - Non-citizen status

## Working with Government Phone Customers
### Common Issues
- Government-assisted cell phones (90-day service)
- Using family/friend's phone for application
- Difficult to reach after call ends

### Solutions
1. Get all documents during initial call
2. Save your number in their contacts immediately
3. Get alternative contact numbers
4. Send reminder texts same day
