# Speed and Efficiency Strategies

## Call Time Targets
### Average Call Duration: 15-20 minutes
- Simple individual: < 7 minutes
- Family of 2: 10-15 minutes
- Large family: 20+ minutes
- Three-way marketplace call: Keep under 20 minutes

**Warning:** "Don't fall into the trap of hour-plus calls"

## Daily Goals
"You want to knock out 20-30 deals in a day.
That's how you make your money.
This is open enrollment - you want to help people,
but streamline the process."

## Speed Strategy
"In enrollment, I complete applications in less than 7 minutes"

### Pre-Application Setup:
**While building rapport:**
1. Ask: "Individual or family coverage?"
2. Say: "Do you have everyone's dates of birth and socials ready?"
3. Write down/type in CRM as you go
4. Start application while asking questions

**Result:** "Application half-done before formally starting"

## Relationship Building for Speed

### Pre-Application Strategy
While building rapport, use pen and paper:
1. "What's everyone's date of birth?" (Write it down)
2. "Are you currently working?" (Start application)
3. "What do you think your income is?" (Keep entering)
4. "Any kids on Medicaid?" (Continue filling)

**Result:** Application half-done before formally starting

## Order-Taking Mindset
### Key Philosophy
"This is ORDER TAKING, not selling"

**Remember:**
- Calls are coming because they WANT health insurance
- They WANT to sign up for healthcare.gov
- You're customer service, helping them
- Don't make them feel like you're selling

### Approach:
"Do you have doctors you already see? 
Let me check that for you.
Certain medications? Let me verify coverage.
Let me find the RIGHT plan for you."

## Key Success Metrics

### Document Collection
- **1-2 out of 10** clients require documents
- **80% success rate** when requested on call
- **<10% success rate** on callbacks

### Common Document Triggers
1. Previous Medicaid application
2. Similar name to existing application
3. Failed to file prior year taxes
4. Income verification needed
5. Immigration status verification

## The Three Rules of Success
1. **Confidence** - Especially for SSN collection
2. **Education** - You're helping, not selling
3. **Persistence** - Get documents NOW, not later

## Final Reminders
"Confidence is everything. 
Read verification, get the yes, keep it pushing.
This is YOUR money - move quick but accurate.
First few apps might drag, but once you realize 
it's repetitive, you should fly right through."
